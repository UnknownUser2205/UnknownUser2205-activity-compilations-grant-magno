Human Adobo

A purely hypothetical culinary exploration of the Filipino classic with a dark twist

⚠️ Warning: This is a fictional recipe ⚠️

The Forbidden Dish

Adobo is considered the national dish of the Philippines, known for its savory, slightly tangy flavor. This version explores what it might be like if the main ingredient were... different. Remember, this is purely a thought experiment and not an actual recipe.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs hypothetical human meat (thighs recommended)
  • 1 cup soy sauce (for ethical marination)
  • 1 cup vinegar (to cleanse the soul)
  • 1 head garlic, crushed (to ward off vampires)
  • 2 bay leaves (for aromatic denial)
  • 1 tbsp peppercorns (to spice up your moral dilemma)
  • 1 cup water (to dilute your guilt)
  • 3 tbsp cooking oil (for frying your conscience)

Preparation

  1. In a large bowl, mix soy sauce and vinegar. Add the theoretical meat and let it marinate for an hour while you reflect on the choices that led you here.
  2. Heat oil in a pot over medium heat. Add garlic and sauté until golden and fragrant—like the last trace of your moral compass.
  3. Add the marinated meat (keep the marinade) and cook until it’s lightly browned on all sides, much like your conscience.
  4. Pour in the reserved marinade, water, bay leaves, and peppercorns. Bring to a boil then reduce heat to simmer.
  5. Cover and cook for 30-40 minutes or until the meat is tender and your soul is sufficiently troubled.
  6. Uncover and simmer until sauce has reduced to your desired consistency.
  7. Serve hot with steamed rice and a side of existential crisis.

Cooking Tips

Ethical Substitutes: If using the main meat bothers you (which it probably should), use pork or chicken instead.

Marination Time: The longer you let the meat sit in the marinade, the more flavor it gets—and the more mixed feelings you might have.

Sauce Consistency: Some people like a lot of sauce, others like it dry. Pick based on how much emotion you want to pour into your food.

Serving Suggestion: Best with a strong drink and thoughts you’ve been avoiding.
